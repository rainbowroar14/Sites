# Tree section stock images

All images are from Wikimedia Commons and are public domain or CC0. make_moss.py cut 10 moss pieces from each of the 3 moss photos and assembled the TOUCH GRASS letters from them (stock/title-*.png). make_stock.py composited the ground strip (stock/ground.png), and make_moss.py pixelated it (stock/ground-px.png). The tree sprites come from rainbowroar14's own Sprout repo, and the signpost is the user's reference image.

## Underground strata (stock/strata.png, built by make_under.py)
Public Domain textures by Titus Tscharntke (via Wikimedia Commons / publicdomainpictures): Ground_dirt.jpg (topsoil), Light_brown_earth_texture_jpg.jpg (clay), Rock_texture_1_jpg.jpg (rock), Stone_rock_texture_closeup_jpg.jpg (deep rock), Small_stones.jpg (pebble cutouts). Roots, worms, fossils and crystals are hand-drawn pixel art.
stock/llc-preview.jpg is a screenshot of https://landrcy.github.io/ that's used as the portal preview.

Public Domain textures by Titus Tscharntke: Ground_dirt.jpg, Light_brown_earth_texture_jpg.jpg, Rock_texture_1_jpg.jpg. Roots and vines are hand-drawn pixel art.

## LLC section (llc/)
The logo, the Binding of Ashaad sprites and screenshot, and the Game of Websites disc all come from https://landrcy.github.io/ (the logo is the site's own rendered monogram canvas). The Eternal Ball Fight image is a screenshot of that site's arena. Card images are downscaled/pixelated copies; the rift cracks are procedural pixel art.
